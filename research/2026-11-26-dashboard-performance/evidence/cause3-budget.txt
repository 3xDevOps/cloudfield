# Cause 3: what holding more lane history costs, and the 7d window after causes 1 and 2

## Browser cost against events held
measure/lanes-memory.ts on a store seeded with --sessions 3000 (346,343 lane events, 46,198 without
rereads), dashboard built with historyBudget raised to 200,000 so each window loads whole, all four
workspaces in scope, no link delay, headless Chromium on one CPU. Heap is JSHeapUsedSize after a
forced collection, minus the same reading on the Board. Second of two runs (the first agreed on
events and requests; its heap readings were 4.6 / n.a. / 8.7 / 15.4 MB).

window  events held  requests  MB fetched  heap MB over Board  script s  all tasks s  settled s

From 4,000 to 33,000 events the heap grows 9.1 MB: about 0.31 KB an event.
